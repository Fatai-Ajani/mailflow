MailFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

MailFlow is an email campaign management tool operated by Fatai Ajani. This policy explains how MailFlow handles information when you connect a Gmail account.

Information we use

MailFlow uses your email address and the OAuth credentials required to send messages through Gmail. It also stores campaign content, contact lists, sending status, and account display settings.

How information is used

Information is used only to connect authorized Gmail accounts, send campaigns you create, manage contacts and templates, and show delivery status.

Google data

MailFlow requests the Gmail send permission and your Google account email address. MailFlow does not request permission to read, search, delete, or modify your mailbox contents. OAuth tokens are stored in the protected application database and are used only for authorized sending.

Sharing and retention

MailFlow does not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the infrastructure and Google services required to operate the application. You may remove a connected account from MailFlow at any time.
